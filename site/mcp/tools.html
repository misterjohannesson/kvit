Kvit MCP · værktøjer

Hvad AI'en kan, og hvad den ikke kan

MCP-serveren har 18 værktøjer: 11 der læser, og 7 der skriver. Denne side er lavet af serveren selv: navne, beskrivelser og argumenter er dem, en assistent ser, når den kobler sig på. Beskrivelserne er på engelsk, fordi assistenten læser dem; dataene er danske.

Tre ting kan den ikke. Udstede fakturaer, oprette kreditnotaer og slette noget. De to første tager numre fra den lovpligtige serie og kan ikke gøres om; sletning hører ikke til i et bogholderi. Der findes heller ikke et værktøj, der redigerer et udstedt dokument eller en logget time. Assistenten kan lave kladden og sige det, men Udsted trykker du selv i programmet.

Alt, der skrives via MCP, står i revisionssporet med afsender api, så du altid kan se, hvad der kom fra AI'en.

Sådan svarer værktøjerne

  • Beløb kommer både som heltal i øre (amount_ore) og formateret (amount_formatted, fx 1.234,56 kr.).
  • Datoer er ISO 8601, kvartaler skrives 2026-Q3.
  • Fejl fra programmet sendes videre som de er, med HTTP-status og programmets egen danske besked. Et 409 på en uforanderlighedsregel bliver til en fejl med retry: false; assistenten bliver bedt om at sige det til dig i stedet for at prøve igen.
  • Serveren præsenterer sig for assistenten med denne instruktion:
Kvit (Faktura) bookkeeping for one Danish business, DKK only. Amounts come as integer øre plus a formatted string; dates are ISO. Read tools answer cash, VAT, P&L and invoice questions; write tools only append reversible records (paid dates, cash movements, reconciliations, drafts, expenses). Issuing and crediting invoices is intentionally not possible via MCP: the owner does that in the app UI.

List invoices

list_invoiceslæser

Invoices and credit notes with number, customer, dates, amounts and status.

With no arguments: open (unpaid, issued) invoices, soonest due first, overdue ones included and flagged.

Answers "what is coming in" and "who is overdue".

Amounts are DKK incl.

VAT unless named otherwise.

Cash position (Balance)

cash_positionlæser

The Balance view: likvider (bank position from the opening balance plus every paid flow), debitorer (open invoices), kreditorer (unpaid expenses), skyldige kreditnotaer, skyldig moms and nettoposition, plus the date of the last reconciliation.

Answers "what is my current bank".

Ingen argumenter.

Prøv: Hvad står jeg i banken?

Cashflow by month

cashflowlæser

Monthly in/out/net and running bank position.

Closed months are actuals (flag "actual"); the current month and every month ahead are "projected": actuals to date plus the forecast of open invoices by due month, unpaid expenses, credit notes to refund and VAT by settlement deadline.

Note: the app has no budget yet, so the projection is not budget-blended; the response says so.

ArgumentTypeKrævetBetydning
months_backheltal (0–120)nejHow many closed months before the current one to include; default 6.
months_forwardheltal (0–24)nejHow many months after the current one; default: every month the forecast reaches.

Prøv: Hvordan ser cashflowet ud frem til nytår?

Reconcile bank balance (afstemning)

reconcile_balanceskriver

The afstemning ritual: give the actual balance from the bank.

The app compares it with computed likvider and, if they differ, books a "correction" cash movement for exactly the difference so likvider matches the bank.

Returns both figures and the delta.

A second call with a matching figure books nothing.

Every call is audit-logged with actor "api" (the booked correction too).

Answers "register my balance today".

ArgumentTypeKrævetBetydning
actual_bank_balance_oreheltaljaThe balance shown by the bank, in integer øre (e.g. 10530500 for 105.305,00 kr.).
datedato (åååå-mm-dd)nejDate of the reconciliation / correction; default today.

Prøv: Registrér min saldo: 84.250 kr. · kan gentages uden at ændre mere · sletter eller ændrer ingenting, der findes

Create draft invoice

create_draft_invoiceskriver

Creates a DRAFT invoice for a customer with the given lines.

Drafts have no number and nothing legal happens.

Issuing (Udsted) and crediting (Opret kreditnota) are intentionally not possible via MCP: they consume numbers from the legal series and are irreversible, so the owner does them in the app UI.

Use list_customers and list_accounts to resolve ids.

Amounts are integer øre ex VAT; VAT (25 %) is computed by the app unless vat_exempt_reason is given.

Audit-logged with actor "api".

Log time (tidsregistrering)

log_timeskriver

Logs hours worked for a customer, to be billed later from the app (or with create_draft_from_time).

Use list_customers to resolve the customer id.

The response includes every entry already logged for that customer on that day, so a repeated call is visible: check possible_duplicate before logging again.

Logged time cannot be edited or deleted via MCP; the owner does that in the app (Timer).

Audit-logged with actor "api".

Create draft invoice from logged time

create_draft_from_timeskriver

Creates a DRAFT invoice holding a customer's unbilled time: all of it, or the given entry ids.

One line per entry at the customer's hourly rate; nothing legal happens and no number is assigned.

The owner then rounds hours, adjusts rates and groups lines under headings on the draft in the app.

Deleting that draft in the app returns the time to unbilled.

Issuing (Udsted) and crediting (Opret kreditnota) are intentionally not possible via MCP: they consume numbers from the legal series and are irreversible, so the owner does them in the app UI.

Audit-logged with actor "api".

ArgumentTypeKrævetBetydning
customer_idheltal (positivt)jaCustomer id from list_customers.
entry_idsliste af heltal (positivt) (1–500)nejOnly these unbilled entries (ids from list_time); default all unbilled billable time of the customer.

Prøv: Lav en kladde med Vestergaards timer fra oktober. · tilføjer en række pr. kald · sletter eller ændrer ingenting, der findes
