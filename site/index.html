KvitFaktura · bogholderi

Bookkeeping for one Danish business, without the drama.

Faktura issues invoices with an unbroken number series, keeps receipts and expenses with their VAT, and turns four quarterly deadlines into the three figures you copy into skat.dk. It runs on your own computer or server, and everything it knows lives in one folder you can copy for backup. An AI assistant can read your numbers and book routine entries through the built-in MCP server, but it can never issue an invoice, create a credit note or delete anything.
